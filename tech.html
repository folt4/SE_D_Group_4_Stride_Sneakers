// BREAKTHROUGH 01 • MIDSOLE ELASTOMER

AeroFoam™ Supercritical Core

Traditional ethylene vinyl acetate (EVA) foams pack out and lose responsiveness after 150 miles. AeroFoam™ is infused with supercritical nitrogen gas under 300 atmospheres of pressure. The result is a cellular lattice that returns 87.4% of impact energy back into forward momentum.

  • 87.4% Energy Rebound: 29% higher than standard racing foams.
  • Featherlight Density: 0.12 g/cm³ density eliminates fatigue over 26.2 miles.
  • All-Temperature Stability: Maintains consistent cushion from -10°C to +40°C.

// BREAKTHROUGH 02 • CARBON PROPULSION

VortexPlate™ Carbon Spine

A flat plate causes calf strain. STRIDE's VortexPlate™ is 3D-sculpted with an ergonomic metatarsal drop and spoon curvature. As your foot rolls from midfoot contact to toe-off, the plate acts as a spring-loaded lever that propels you forward into the next stride cycle.

  • Aerospace 3K Weave: Zero torsional twisting on sharp turns.
  • Variable Stiffness Tuning: Stiff under the big toe for pop, flexible at lateral edges for smooth cornering.

// BREAKTHROUGH 03 • SUSTAINABLE KNITTING

BioKnit™ Seamless Upper

Woven as a single continuous piece on computerized jacquard looms. By altering yarn density millimeter by millimeter, we achieve lockdown hold around the midfoot, ventilated air channels over the forefoot, and zero internal seams to eliminate blisters.

  • 92% Less Material Waste: Engineered to the exact shoe shape without cut-off scraps.
  • Hydrophobic Treatment: Repels sweat and puddle splashes without clogging air ventilation.
