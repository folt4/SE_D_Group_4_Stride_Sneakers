Brand-Specific Fit Recommendations

Air Jordan 1 & 4 TRUE TO SIZE

Order your standard US sneaker size. Jordan 4s may feel slightly snug around the pinky toe during initial break-in, but leather adapts quickly.

Nike SB Dunk Low HALF SIZE UP (+0.5)

Due to the extra padded puffy tongue and Zoom Air sockliner, SB Dunks fit narrower than regular retro Dunks. We recommend going +0.5 up.

Adidas Yeezy 350 V2 HALF SIZE UP (+0.5)

The shallow toe box in Primeknit creates front toe pressure. Going a half size up (+0.5) guarantees ideal comfort and daily wearability.

Balenciaga Triple S FULL SIZE DOWN (-1.0)

European luxury cut. Triple S silhouettes run significantly oversized. We advise sizing down by 1 full EU size (e.g. EU 44 → EU 43).

How to Measure Your Feet at Home (3 Steps)

1

Tape Paper to Floor

Stand upright with your heel firmly pressed against a flat wall on top of a sheet of white paper.

2

Mark Heel & Longest Toe

Using a pencil held strictly vertical, trace the furthest tip of your longest toe and mark the back of the heel.

3

Measure Length

Measure the distance between marks in centimeters. Use our interactive slider above to look up your perfect size.
